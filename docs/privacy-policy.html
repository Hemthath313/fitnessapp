Privacy Policy – Gym Buddy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Gym Buddy (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is developed and operated by Hemthath, an individual developer based in India. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: buddyspps204@gmail.com

1. Data we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us).
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address and Google account ID.

Profile and fitness information you enter

  • Name, age, height, gender, body weight, goal weight and fitness goal.
  • Body measurements (for example chest, waist, hips).
  • Workouts, exercises, sets, reps, weights, personal records and workout history.
  • Meals, calories and water intake.
  • Programme progress, streaks, badges and challenge feedback.
  • Feedback or support messages you send us.

Data collected automatically

  • Crash reports and diagnostic data (device model, OS version, app version, error details).
  • App usage analytics (screens opened and features used).
  • Advertising ID and ad interaction data (free version only).
  • Subscription status and purchase details from Google Play (we never receive your payment card details).

Data stored only on your device

  • Reminder settings and scheduled notifications, unit preference (kg/lb), and data shown in the home-screen widget.

We do not access your camera, photos, contacts, microphone or location.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save your workouts, nutrition and progress and sync them across your devices.
  • To personalise programmes, weight suggestions, streaks and reminders.
  • To provide and verify Gym Buddy Pro subscriptions.
  • To show ads in the free version.
  • To fix crashes and improve the app.
  • To respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-party services

Gym Buddy uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

Service Purpose
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, Analytics, App Check, Cloud Messaging) Accounts, data storage, crash reports, analytics, security, notifications
Google Sign-In Optional sign-in with your Google account

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device’s Advertising ID to show relevant ads. If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it any time under More → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings. Gym Buddy Pro has no ads.

5. Health and fitness data

Your fitness and body data is used only to provide the app’s features. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with ad networks.

6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers, which may be located outside India. Data is encrypted in transit, and access is restricted so that each user can read and write only their own data. No method of storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: More → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and your workouts, logs, meals, water, measurements, weight history, statistics and programme data.
  • By email: write to buddyspps204@gmail.com from your registered email address, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account; use one of the options above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent, and raise a complaint. Most of your data can be viewed and edited directly in the app. For anything else, email buddyspps204@gmail.com.

9. Children

Gym Buddy is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date at the top. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
